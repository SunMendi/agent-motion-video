# Direction — 5 Secret Free AI Tools Showcase

## 1. Outcome and Audience
This video presents 5 high-impact free AI tools to Bangladeshi students, creators, and professionals.
Viewers learn how each tool solves real daily problems in productivity, content, and audio.

## 2. Visual Treatment and Aesthetics
We use a modern dark glass aesthetic inspired by top product motion design studios.
- Background: Deep slate navy (`#0B0F17`) with a subtle radial cyan glow and grid pattern.
- Staging: 9:16 vertical canvas (1080x1920) optimized for Reels and Shorts.
- Real Product UI: We embed the authentic user-uploaded screenshots inside sleek browser mockup frames.
- Depth & Motion: Cards have 3D perspective tilt (`rotateX`, `rotateY`), specular edge highlights, and layered shadows.
- Physics: We use calibrated harmonic spring physics (`mass: 0.8`, `damping: 15`, `stiffness: 110`) for smooth, tactile card entrances.

## 4. Top Captions and Typography
- Placement: Safe zone Y = 100 to 260 px with 160 px height.
- Typography: Hind Siliguri and Noto Sans Bengali.
- Display: Frosted dark glass container with glowing cyan active word accents.
- Body Rule: Subtitles provide full speech. Stage graphics show only short 1 to 3 word labels.

## 5. Sound Design and Audio
- Voice: Preserved `public/current/freeaai.wav` with ducked background music bed.
- SFX: Tactile Foley effects (clicks, whooshes, pops, pings, camera shutters) lock to exact keyframe impacts.
- Mix Targets: Integrated loudness between -16 and -14 LUFS; true peak at or below -1 dBTP.
