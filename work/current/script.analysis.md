- **Bengali (Spoken)**:

  - যেগুলা PDF বুঝবে, Presentation বানাবে,
  - এমনকি তোমার ভয়েসও পরিষ্কার করবে।
  - এই Before-After শুনলে পার্থক্যটা নিজেই বুঝবে।
- **English Translation**:
  - These 5 AI tools will speed up your daily workflow.
  - They understand PDFs, create presentations,

  - You will hear the difference immediately in this before-and-after comparison.
- **Narrative Function**: Grabs attention with high-impact utility promises and an audio demo hook.

### Section 2: Tool 1 – Napkin AI (13.00s – 28.80s)
- **Bengali (Spoken)**:
  - এক নম্বর, Napkin AI।
  - কোনো আইডিয়া বোঝাতে গিয়ে বিশাল প্যারাগ্রাফ লিখে ফেলছো?

## 5. Design and Production Notes

1. **Top Captions**:
   - Keep dynamic captions at the top safe area as defined in `src/shared/video-layout.json`.
   - Never duplicate full narrative text inside the stage body.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**:
   - Display the tool screenshot or animated mock interface prominently in the middle canvas (y: 350px to 1450px).
   - Anchor the 1–3 word key phrase labels near the focal action (e.g., adjacent to source links or diagram boxes).
3. **Pacing and Transitions**:
   - Total runtime is 84.96 seconds (~2548 frames at 30 fps).
   - Use swift slide transitions (10–12 frames) between tools to maintain viewer retention.
