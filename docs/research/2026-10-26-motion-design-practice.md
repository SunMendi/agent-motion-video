# Professional motion design practice and the local design-lead system

Research date: 2026-10-26. Read first-party studio accounts and practitioner articles. These sources inform the process; they do not endorse this workspace or establish an automatic quality ranking.

## Sources and useful findings

- [Ordinary Folk: process](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/process). The studio describes understanding the message, choosing a visual treatment, developing animation, and considering audio throughout. Local adaptation: strategy and art direction precede detailed scene implementation; sound has a communicative purpose from the start.
- [Joyce N. Ho: concepting and pitching](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/concepting-and-pitching-ideas-to-clients). Ho discusses interpreting the brief, exploring ideas, selecting a concept, and connecting a narrative metaphor with visual and motion references. Local adaptation: the lead chooses and explains a concept instead of asking the user to choose among styles.
- [Simon Rankin: motion design development](https://www.simonrankin.com/learn/2020/07/28/the-motion-design-development-process-thumbnails-storyboards-style-boards-and-animatics). Rankin distinguishes thumbnails, storyboards, style frames, and animatics by the decisions each makes visible. Local adaptation: composition, appearance, sequence, and timing need actual visual proof rather than completed prose fields.
- [Jake Bartlett: animatics](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/what-are-animatics-and-why-are-they-important). Bartlett describes a rough sequence timed to voice/music, with detail proportional to the project. Local adaptation: use the current recording to test rhythm and repair sequence problems before detailed asset animation; avoid a fixed quota of polished preproduction artifacts.
- [BUCK: Airbnb case study](https://buck.co/work/airbnb-story). BUCK describes adapting technique to supplied references and a difficult schedule, including experimenting with 3D previs for a cel-animation result. Local adaptation: choose a feasible production approach that preserves the visual intent; the desired look should not automatically determine a single implementation technique.

## Synthesis implemented here

The maintained entry point is `design-lead.md`. It separates strategy, concept, visual language, sequence planning, worker handoff, and rendered critique. `audio-to-visual-director.md` retains scene craft. `workflow.md` retains production mechanics. `templates/direction.md` structures the existing direction record rather than introducing another JSON gate.

The format table is our own design aid, not a studio's universal formula. Ads prioritize relevant value and proof; education prioritizes understanding; demos prioritize visible interaction; documentary prioritizes evidence; stories prioritize emotional progression. The chosen audience and outcome decide which priorities apply. Techniques can mix.

The role split is a local adaptation: a separate design lead briefs the user's worker, or one agent performs both passes for full production. Studio client-review stages are adapted to the user's requirement for autonomous execution. Only changes to supplied material need permission; routine concept selection and sample critique are internal work.

Current workspace constraints remain: one application, 1080×1920 vertical output, top captions, preserved recording, current-input content boundaries, available tools and authorized budget, and truthful review evidence. The WhatsApp explainer is a supplied style reference for the current request, not a global template for future genres.

## Behavior to verify during actual productions

The lead should produce distinct, justified approaches for a product ad, a novice lesson, and a software demo. The worker should receive concrete subjects/actions and observable sample criteria. The lead should diagnose a failed render at the responsible layer, with exact evidence and a specific repair. Testing file structure or syntax alone does not prove these creative decisions; observing future outputs remains necessary.

No article was used to authorize installing new software, modifying narration, inventing source evidence, or adding user approval checkpoints. No model training, autonomous external worker service, or guaranteed aesthetic score is claimed.
